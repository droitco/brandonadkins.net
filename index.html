Bio

Brandon Adkins is the President of Droit, LLC, a self-storage company based in Eau Claire, Wisconsin that operates under the Smart Self Storage name. Droit develops sites, builds stores, operates them, and manages stores for other owners.

Droit has ten stores open across seven states: Wisconsin, Minnesota, Iowa, Ohio, Tennessee, Texas, and Washington. Every open store has 24/7 gate access, and rentals are completed online on each store's own website.

Droit also works with independent self-storage owners. Its third-party management covers inbound calls, collections, gate access, and the lien process, and it builds websites for independent facilities. The owner stays the landlord and approves any spend.
